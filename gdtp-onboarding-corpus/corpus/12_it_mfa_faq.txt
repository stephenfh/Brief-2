Q1. What is MFA and why do I need it?
MFA means you prove who you are with two things: something you know (your password) and
something you have (your hardware token or the authenticator app). It is required for VPN and
for any system holding programme data.

Q2. How do I get a hardware token?
Tokens are ordered by the IT Service Desk when your provisioning ticket is raised. Tokens
normally arrive within 3 to 5 working days of ordering, although couriers occasionally take
longer around public holidays. You will get an email from the service desk when the token has
been dispatched.

Q3. How do I activate my token?
Activation is done in person at the Service Desk (Dublin, 2nd floor) with photo ID. Bring the
token and your laptop. It takes about ten minutes. Tokens cannot be activated remotely.

Q4. I haven't received my token. What should I do?
Check the ticket linked in your welcome pack first. If the stated ETA has passed, contact the
Service Desk at servicedesk@gdtp.example quoting the ticket number.

Q5. I lost my token / it stopped working.
Report it to the Service Desk straight away. We will disable the old token and issue a
replacement. A replacement normally takes 3 to 5 working days; a temporary code can be issued in
person in the meantime.

Q6. Can I use my phone instead of a hardware token?
Authenticator-app MFA is available for corporate laptops only. Roles with production access are
issued a hardware token.
