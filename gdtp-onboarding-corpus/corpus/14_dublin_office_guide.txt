DUBLIN OFFICE - VISITOR AND PARKING GUIDE
Facilities Management            Updated: 3 Aug 2026

Getting here
The Dublin office is a ten-minute walk from the nearest Luas stop and about fifteen minutes from
the main bus corridor. Cycle racks are located at the basement entrance, with lockers and a drying
room beside them.

Reception and visitors
Reception is staffed from 8:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday. All visitors must sign in, show photo
ID and wear a visitor badge at all times. Please let your host know in advance so a pass can be
prepared. Visitors must be escorted beyond the ground floor.

Parking
Staff parking is limited and allocated by the Facilities team. There are 40 permit spaces in the
basement car park; permits are issued on a first-come basis and reviewed every quarter. Visitor
parking is available on level -1 (six spaces) and must be booked via facilities@gdtp.example at
least one working day ahead. The car park barrier closes at 20:00. There is no overnight parking.
Electric vehicle charging: four bays on level -2, bookable through the Facilities portal.

Meeting rooms
Rooms on floors 1 to 3 can be booked through the calendar. Catering for meetings of more than
eight people should be ordered 48 hours ahead.

Accessibility
Step-free access is available at the side entrance on the east side of the building. Lifts serve
all floors. Please tell Facilities if you need any adjustments for a visit.

Emergencies
In an emergency follow the green signs to the assembly point at the rear car park. Fire wardens
wear yellow vests. First-aiders are listed beside each lift.
